Who We Are

Premium Ethiopian Coffee for Local & Global Markets

Misrak Coffee is dedicated to sourcing, processing, roasting, and exporting exceptional Ethiopian Arabica coffee while preserving the rich heritage and unique flavor profiles that make Ethiopian coffee world-renowned.
